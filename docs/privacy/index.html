Privacy policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Overview

Crop & Convert to WEBP is designed to work locally in your browser.

The extension does not require account creation, does not use analytics, does not send your images to external servers, and does not sell or share personal data.

What data the extension accesses

The extension accesses only the image files that you explicitly select or drag into the popup interface for processing.

  • image file contents
  • image file names shown in the popup while processing
  • image format information needed to crop or convert the file

This information is used only to generate local previews and the output files you request.

What data the extension stores

Crop & Convert to WEBP does not store persistent personal data in remote services or browser storage as part of its normal workflow.

Selected images are processed in memory inside your browser session and the generated files are offered back to you as downloads under your control.

Downloads and generated files

When you crop or convert an image, the extension generates a processed file locally in the browser and provides a download link so you can save it to your device.

Once downloaded, the resulting file is handled according to where you store, copy, or share it.

What the extension does not do

Crop & Convert to WEBP does not:

  • collect account details
  • collect payment information
  • collect browsing history
  • record page contents
  • track keystrokes
  • upload images to a remote server
  • send telemetry or analytics events
  • use advertising SDKs
  • sell personal information

Permissions and why they are needed

This extension does not request Chrome extension permissions in its current manifest.

It relies on standard browser capabilities available to a popup page to let you choose local image files, preview them, crop them, convert them, and download the output.

Data sharing

The extension does not share your files or processed outputs with the developer or third parties.

Your control

You decide which files to process and whether to download the generated results. Closing the popup or removing the extension stops future access to files selected through the extension.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the extension’s functionality or data practices change. The latest version of the policy should be published alongside the project and any store listing used for distribution.
